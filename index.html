DSPIRA

Digital Signal Processing in Radio Astronomy (DSPIRA) began as an NSF Research Experiences for Teachers site in the Lane Department of Computer Science and Electrical Engineering at West Virginia University. This short video is a quick introduction.

The program gave teachers the training and materials to work in radio astronomy. The {{ lesson_count }} free lessons here were designed by the participating teachers themselves, and they cover radio astronomy from building a telescope to operating it.

You can try a classroom activity with no telescope, build a telescope or start observing with one you have. If you are planning a class, see Teach with DSPIRA.
